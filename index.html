Your contribution provides fruit trees to students across Bangladesh. 100% Transparency. 100% Legacy.

Transparency & Impact

Why Fruit Trees?

We combine nutrition with nature. Every tree planted provides food for a child and oxygen for the planet.

Where is the money?

100% of donor money goes to sapling purchase. Admin and labor costs are managed by our internal funds.

Who gets the trees?

We target schools and students in climate-vulnerable areas of Bangladesh first.

How to Track?

Every donor gets a plot in the Ark Archive Grid, linked to the real-world planting location.

MISSION & DEFENSE

Shield Against Environmental Impact

Modern global instability releases toxic gases, chemical residues, and massive dust into the atmosphere. This leads to disasters like "oily rain" and respiratory threats.

Our trees act as biological filters. By planting millions, we build a natural shield for our children. Preparedness through nature is the only way to counteract the invisible damage of global instability.

Our Goal & Priority

We aim to provide every student in Bangladesh from Class 1 to 10 with their own fruit tree. Once achieved, we expand globally to restore ecological balance.
